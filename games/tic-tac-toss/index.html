How to play

Tic Tac Toss

  1. Choose one player against the CPU or two players on the same device.
  2. Grab your colored piece from its container and drag it over an open square.
  3. Release to snap the piece into place. Get three in a row to win.

If a toss misses the grid, grab the same piece and try again. In one-player mode, the CPU places a blue O after your red X.
